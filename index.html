where
the garden, a forest near porto, portugal

rainy autumn forest vibes, a hot tub and a sauna, dancing and music, long talks, cuddles, catching up, and a private chef for the end of the weekend. you fly in from wherever you are and stay as long as you want.

the idea

it's been a wandering, inward-turned two years, and i haven't seen any of you as much as i'd like. i want to take these few days to reconnect and care for each other.

there are a ton of instruments, so we can jam and sing. there's a projector for karaoke or movies. i won't be making a program. it's co-created: music, games, stories, art, rituals, relating, any little creative touch you want to add is welcome. let's make it a cozy, fun time together.

this isn't a produced event, and the garden isn't usually open in winter, so expect some figuring it out as we go. it's as comfortable as we make it.

i'd love to have you here.

the place

9 hectares of forest near porto, with a lake, two springs, and mossy boulders everywhere.

the cork room

decked out with mattresses, cushions, soft things and tea. this is our reliably warm haven to come back to whenever it gets cold or rainy outside. if too many people want to come, it could also become an extra dorm or co-sleeping room at night.

the dojo

the big hall, full of instruments, looking out into the trees. the gym is on the other side of it.

the kitchen

ours to use. for snacks, tea, or cooking something of your own. the big meals come from the main kitchen.

sleeping

a dorm, little cabins, a yurt, and rooms in the old stone guest villa. in winter all the glamping comes down, so beds are very limited. if you have a van or another way to sleep, that's super easy, and it means more people can come.

food and chores

until the chef arrives, we'll figure food out together. for each meal, one person takes the kitchen lead with a nice vision for it, and others help. if you love to cook and would enjoy taking on one of the meals, let me know. i'm thinking two meals a day, brunch and dinner, with self-serve snacks in between.

we'll also share some chores: washing dishes, keeping the communal spaces tidy, and feeding the hot tub and sauna fires with wood.

what it costs

nothing. your bed and your meals are my gift to you. you just get yourself here.

i don't want cost to be a limiting factor for anyone. if flights are hard to afford, reach out to me.

if you're financially comfortable and want to support, i'll gratefully accept.

you can also stay past tuesday (for days or weeks) at discounted garden rates. i'll be here until early-mid december, and would love to have a small crew here for longer. reach out to me to arrange this.

getting there

fly into porto (OPO). a taxi from the airport to the garden is about €45, so it's worth sharing one. once you say yes, i'll send the exact address and try to match people up by arrival time.

weather

late november here is mild and wet. over the last ten years, these days were usually 13–18° during the day and 6–13° at night. about half of them had rain. some years it poured all weekend, some years it was dry and sunny.

on average there are about 6–7 hours of sunshine a day, so depending on where you're coming from, you might actually get to recharge on sun a bit.

the garden is normally closed in winter, and the big halls are cold and hard to heat. so we'll hang out in the cork room, which is usually used for yoga and meditation. there's also a big kitchen in the stone house with a wood stove, and the cabins have electric radiators. the rest of the warmth is in the sauna and the hot tub. dress for potential cold in between.

photos and videos

i love documenting memories and would love to have audiovisual or other sensory evidence of our time together (olfactory, for example, is an option). so please take photos and videos whenever you feel like it or see something beautiful. i'll make a shared folder for everyone to upload to, and it all stays within the group.

we can agree on a photo policy together. if you'd rather not be photographed, let me know.

are you coming?

a plus one is possible, especially if you're sharing a bed with them. please talk to me beforehand.
